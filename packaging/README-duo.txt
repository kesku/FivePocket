FivePocket Duo {version}
========================

For the iPhone Duo inner display. A Mac with Apple silicon and Xcode builds the
app from your own Xbox 360 Install and Play disc images. No game is included.

INSTALL
-------
1. Build with the Mac Duo builder, then sign and install the IPA using your own
   Apple identity. Keep the builder's bundle identifier for every reinstall so
   the app retains its imported data and saves. Do not delete the existing app
   before reinstalling. A free Apple ID may require re-signing every seven days.
2. Enable Developer Mode in Settings > Privacy & Security and trust your signing
   identity under Settings > General > VPN & Device Management when requested.
3. Keep about 18 GB free for imported files, or about 36 GB while both ISO images
   and imported files occupy the device. Copy the images into Files > On My iPhone
   > FivePocket Duo, or choose them from a USB drive with Choose A Disc Image.
4. Open the app and import both discs. If interrupted, start import again; verified
   files already copied are reused. After both discs finish, delete the ISO copies
   from the app folder to recover their space. Keep your originals on your Mac.

CONTROLS
--------
Portrait split puts the game above the map, wheels and phone. Landscape or Lower
Screen Off uses the full display. A gamepad is the main control; touch operates
menus and the lower screen. Touch controls hide while a gamepad is connected.

The Top Screen menu offers Fit, Zoom, Stretch and 4:3. Fit preserves the full
picture; Zoom crops to fill; Stretch fills without cropping. The 4:3 game layout
requires a restart. The other choices apply immediately and persist.

Hold LB, D-pad Left or D-pad Down to select from the lower-screen wheels. Tap the
map for a waypoint. On the split pause map, drag the upper screen to pan and pinch
to zoom. During a call, tap the lower screen's left half to answer or right half
to decline. A left-edge swipe opens the side menu; the full-screen button or a
two-finger double tap switches the display.
